# Heat pump efficiency

Heat pump efficiency is measured by the coefficient of performance (COP): units of heat delivered per unit of electricity used. Because a heat pump moves heat rather than generating it, a COP well above 1 is normal.

Electric resistance heaters, by comparison, have a COP of about 1, since every unit of electricity becomes one unit of heat.

COP is not fixed. It falls as the temperature difference between the heat source and the home grows, so the same unit is less efficient on the coldest days of the year.
